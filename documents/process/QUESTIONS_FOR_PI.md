## 7. Directionality & causality — the deepest gap (future work, worth planning now)

*Context: everything above establishes that tumour CTGF is **associated with** and **spatially co-localised with** myCAF/TGF-β activation, and NicheNet ranks CTGF a top predicted driver — but "associated + co-localised + predicted" is not causal. Below is how I think we close that, and where I'd like your steer. A key conceptual point I want to raise: these are **two different "directionalities"** and they need different tools.*

18. **Temporal precedence (the "who makes CTGF first" argument).** My intuition: in *early* disease the mature myCAF pool hasn't formed yet, so an initiating CTGF signal plausibly comes from the transformed epithelium, with CAFs later becoming the dominant (autocrine-amplified) source — which would reconcile "CAFs dominate CTGF in resected tumours" with "the tumour initiates." **Caveat I want to stress-test with you:** normal pancreas already has quiescent stellate cells (PSCs) that *themselves* make CTGF on activation, and desmoplasia begins at the **PanIN** stage — so "the early CTGF is tumour-derived" is a *hypothesis to test*, not a given. To test it we'd need a **grade/stage- or precursor-resolved cohort** (normal → PanIN → invasive, or histologic grade metadata) and ask whether epithelial CTGF precedes/predicts CAF activation along that axis. **Question:** is this worth a dedicated aim, and do you know a cohort with early lesions/clinical grade we can access? (Our current datasets are resected/advanced; in-hand proxies for a progression axis are only malignant-cell CNV burden, classical→basal score, or pseudotime — all observational and patient-confounded, so suggestive at best.)

19. **Spatial source attribution (a *different* axis, and the cheap in-hand fix).** This is **not** the temporal question — it asks *where* the CTGF is made and whether tumour-CTGF sits next to activated CAFs. Our Visium arm is "source-ambiguous" only because each spot is a cell mixture. **RCTD + C-SIDE (`spacexr`, already a dependency)** deconvolve each spot into cell-type fractions and then test cell-type-specific expression — i.e. "is CTGF expressed by the *malignant* compartment specifically, and is malignant-CTGF elevated in spots adjacent to activated-CAF spots?" That directly converts our "co-localised" verdict into "tumour-sourced, paracrine-proximal." **Question:** is closing this expected for the target journal (my read of point 11), and are you happy for me to run RCTD/C-SIDE on the existing GSE311783 data as the next concrete step? (Single-cell spatial — Xenium/MERFISH/Visium HD — would settle source without deconvolution, if a cohort is reachable.)

20. **The actual causal test = perturbation.** Neither #18 nor #19 proves causality; both are observational. The gold standard is **CTGF knockdown/knockout in tumour organoids co-cultured with PSCs/CAFs**, asking whether myCAF/TGF-β activation drops. **Question:** do you want to scope a wet-lab collaboration for this (turning the paper into a functional study), or is the honest ceiling for *this* manuscript "consistent-with, triangulated across source + receptor competence + activation + replication + spatial co-localisation + network prediction," with #18–#20 framed as future directions? This ties back to point 1 (how hard we push on causality).
